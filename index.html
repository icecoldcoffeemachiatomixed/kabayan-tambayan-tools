    Built like a tambayan

    A tambayan is the place where everyone gathers. This hub works the same way: every tool sits in one shared workspace, shares one look, and runs without sending you elsewhere.

    1 · Connect
    Open a card and paste the URL of that project's deployed app, or set it once for everyone in the project manifest.
    2 · Launch
    The app loads inside its card. Reload it, go fullscreen or pop it out to a new tab from the card's toolbar.
    3 · Stay in the tambayan
    Open several apps at once and switch between them without leaving the hub.
